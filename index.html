The Route

One long loop, anticlockwise

Narita across to the Hida mountains, down to the Toyama Bay coast, east along the Sea of Japan, up into Myōkō for three nights, then a last stop in Kawagoe before the flight home. Tap a leg to trace it.

What To Pack

Layers, not a suitcase full of separate outfits

Average highs and lows across the trip run from about −4° to 13°C, indoors are heated and often warm, and you'll be in and out of ryokan, cars, and snow all day. Clothing only — ski jackets, boards, and lift-ready gear are covered by the rental shops on the ski days.

Layer, don't bulk up. Days swing roughly 10°C between morning and afternoon, and you'll move between a heated car, a heated ryokan, and the outdoors repeatedly — several thin layers you can add or shed beat one heavy coat. Hand and foot warmers (kairo, カイロ) are sold everywhere in convenience stores if you run short.
